## Scoring Methodology

Each dimension is scored 0-100:

| Dimension | What's Measured | Weight |
|---|---|---|
| **Title Tag** | Length 50-60 chars, primary keyword up front, brand suffix, uniqueness across site | 20% |
| **Meta Description** | Length 150-160 chars, keyword inclusion, CTA, uniqueness | 15% |
| **Heading Structure** | H1 matches topic, logical H2-H6 hierarchy, keywords in subheadings | 15% |
| **Content Depth** | Word count (>1500 for guides, >800 for service pages), topic completeness, LSI keywords, E-E-A-T signals | 25% |
| **Internal Linking** | Cross-links to related services/guides, contextual anchors, hub-spoke architecture | 15% |
| **URL Structure** | Clean slug, keyword inclusion, proper hierarchy (/learn/topic-name/), no parameters | 10% |

**Overall SEO = weighted average of above.**

## SITE-WIDE ON-PAGE SEO ISSUES

### Critical (Score Impact: -15 to -25 points)

1. **Templated City Pages** — 14 location pages appear to use the same content template with city-name swaps. Google's Helpful Content Update penalizes thin/duplicate location pages. Each needs 30-40% unique content minimum.

2. **Title Tag Problems** — Homepage title "Virtual Auditor — Startup Valuation" ignores 7 of 9 service verticals. ALL CAPS used on guide titles. Several titles exceed 60 characters and get truncated in SERPs.

3. **Missing FAQPage Schema on /faq** — The site has a 50+ FAQ page with zero structured data. This is the single highest-ROI fix for SERP visibility and AEO.

4. **Duplicate Content: `/learn/register-a-company-online-2/`** — The "-2" suffix indicates a WordPress duplicate. Must consolidate or 301 redirect.

### High (Score Impact: -10 to -15 points)

5. **Service/Educational Path Confusion** — Commercial pages like `/learn/startup-valuation-service/` and `/learn/business-valuation-services-in-bangalore/` sit inside the educational `/learn/` path, creating keyword cannibalization and confusing search intent signals.

6. **Inconsistent Trailing Slashes** — Some URLs use trailing slashes (`/about-us/`), others don't (`/contact-us`). This creates duplicate URL issues.

7. **Meta Description Weakness** — Most meta descriptions appear generic/templated. No pages show strong CTAs, unique value props, or trust signals in SERP snippets.

8. **Date Freshness** — `/learn/company-registration-bangalore/` still says "2025" in its title. Learning Centre articles need quarterly freshness updates.

### Medium (Score Impact: -5 to -10 points)

9. **Overly Long URL Slugs** — 8 URLs exceed 50 characters in the slug portion. Shorten without losing keywords.

10. **Missing Author Bylines** — Learning Centre articles reference "Registered Valuer" credentials but lack visible author attribution with structured Person markup.

11. **Thin Internal Linking on City Pages** — Location pages average only 35-40/100 on internal linking. Need contextual links to related services, guides, and tools.

12. **Founding Year Inconsistency** — Homepage says "since 2012"; private-limited page says "since 2017". Google devalues E-E-A-T when factual claims are contradictory across pages.
