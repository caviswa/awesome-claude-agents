## Critical Finding: The Template Location Page Problem

The site has ~30+ location pages (audit in HSR Layout, audit in Andheri, valuation in Surat, etc.) that follow the same template with the city name swapped. This is a **major E-E-A-T and Helpful Content risk**.

**Current pattern (scoring ~44/100):**
> "[Service] in [City] — Virtual Auditor provides [boilerplate service description]. Starting from ₹[price]. Contact us for a free consultation."

**100/100 pattern requires:**
> Unique local content per page: local ROC/tax authority details, state-specific stamp duty and fee tables, regional industry context, a named local case study, local team member (even a point-of-contact name), photos of the local office, local Google Map embed, state-specific regulatory citations, and locally relevant FAQ.
